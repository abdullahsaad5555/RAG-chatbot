# 01 | Governance, scope and interpretation

## 01.1 Purpose and application

The Company shall maintain consistent employment practices that support fair treatment, competent staffing and reliable operations. This manual applies to direct employees unless a clause expressly limits eligibility. Trainees, secondees and contractor personnel shall receive a written statement identifying which site and conduct requirements apply to them. Contractor personnel shall not automatically receive direct-employee benefits.

## 01.2 Policy hierarchy

HR and Legal shall resolve conflicts between this manual, applicable law, approved work regulations and employment contracts before a decision is implemented. No clause shall be interpreted to remove a mandatory protection or an enforceable contractual entitlement. A more favorable contractual term shall not be reduced merely because a lower sample benefit appears in this manual.

## 01.3 Language and access

The approved manual shall be accessible to employees in a language they understand. Legal shall confirm required Arabic documentation and the controlling-language position. HR shall maintain synchronized translations, version numbers and accessible copies for employees without routine computer access.

## 01.4 Control and review

HR shall maintain the master copy, change log and policy register. Material changes require consultation with affected functions, legal review and approval by the designated authority. Changes shall identify their effective date, transition arrangements and communication plan. Employee acknowledgment confirms receipt, not waiver of rights.

Control record: policy owner - HR; approval - designated authority; evidence - relevant request, assessment, approval and employee communication.

# 02 | Roles, authority and exceptions

## 02.1 Responsibilities

Employees shall provide accurate information, follow approved procedures and raise errors promptly. Managers shall apply rules consistently, plan staffing and document decisions. HR shall interpret policy, maintain records and monitor consistency. Finance shall validate financial controls and payroll reconciliations. Legal shall advise on legal risk. HSE and Operations shall determine technical safety and competence requirements within their mandates.

## 02.2 Decision authority

No manager may promise employment, salary, promotion, reimbursement or benefits without the required approval. The final delegation of authority shall specify role titles, financial limits, substitute approvers and prohibited combinations of duties. A person shall not approve their own request, a payment to themselves or a decision involving an unmanaged personal conflict.

## 02.3 Exceptions

A request for exception shall state the rule, business reason, affected employee category, cost, duration, alternatives and consistency implications. HR shall check precedent; Finance shall review financial impact; Legal shall review legal exposure where relevant. The authorized approver shall record approval or rejection and reasons. Exceptions shall expire on the stated date and shall not silently become permanent policy.

## 02.4 Records

HR shall retain the request, supporting assessment, approval, communication and expiry review in the exception register. Urgent verbal direction shall be documented promptly and shall not substitute for mandatory legal or safety controls.

Control record: policy owner - HR; approval - designated authority; evidence - relevant request, assessment, approval and employee communication.

# 03 | Workforce planning and job architecture

## 03.1 Annual planning

Each function shall submit a workforce plan identifying current positions, expected departures, critical vacancies, required skills and proposed changes. Operations shall assess minimum competent coverage for operating units and planned maintenance periods. HR shall consolidate the plan and Finance shall confirm budget availability before approval.

## 03.2 Position control

Every position shall have an approved identifier, reporting line, location, employment category, job description and grade. A vacancy does not itself authorize recruitment. New positions and material changes require organization and budget approval. HR shall maintain a position register and reconcile it to payroll and recruitment records.

## 03.3 Job evaluation

Job evaluation shall assess the role rather than the current employee. Factors shall include accountability, required competence, problem-solving and working conditions using an approved method. Managers shall not inflate titles to bypass grading controls. Employees may request clarification through HR; an independent reviewer shall consider material factual errors.

## 03.4 Critical-role continuity

Functions shall identify roles whose vacancy or competence gap could materially affect safe operations or business continuity. Each shall have a coverage plan, development actions and an escalation owner. Temporary coverage shall not authorize an unqualified person to perform restricted work.

Control record: policy owner - HR; approval - designated authority; evidence - relevant request, assessment, approval and employee communication.

# 04 | Recruitment and selection

## 04.1 Requisition and sourcing

Recruitment shall begin only after an approved requisition confirms the position, grade, budget, selection criteria and employment category. HR shall determine suitable sourcing channels and applicable localization obligations with Legal. Internal applicants shall receive fair consideration against published role requirements.

## 04.2 Assessment

Interview panels shall use job-related questions and a consistent scoring framework. Technical assessments shall reflect actual role requirements. Panel members shall disclose personal relationships or other conflicts before reviewing candidates. HR shall retain the selection rationale, not merely the preferred candidate name.

## 04.3 Verification and privacy

Qualification, reference and other lawful checks shall be proportionate to the role and conducted with required notices and permissions. Adverse information shall be assessed for relevance and accuracy; the candidate shall have a reasonable opportunity to clarify discrepancies. Candidate information shall be accessible only to authorized participants.

## 04.4 Offer and closure

Only HR or another formally authorized signatory may issue an offer. The approved package shall match the grade and budget or carry a documented exception. Conditions shall be explicit and not changed informally. Unsuccessful applicants shall be informed appropriately. Recruitment agencies shall operate under approved contracts and lawful fee arrangements.

Control record: policy owner - HR; approval - designated authority; evidence - relevant request, assessment, approval and employee communication.

# 05 | Contracts, probation and onboarding

## 05.1 Contract controls

HR shall arrange a legally reviewed contract stating the employer, role, location, employment term, pay components, working arrangements and applicable conditions. Required employment-system registration and authorization checks shall be completed by the responsible function. Employees shall receive their documents and an opportunity to ask questions before acceptance.

## 05.2 Probation management

Probation may be used only on legally valid and expressly documented terms. HR shall confirm the permissible duration, exclusions, extensions and notice requirements before use. The manager shall set role expectations at joining and hold documented progress reviews. A proposed confirmation, extension or separation shall reach HR before the relevant deadline; silence shall not be used as a substitute for a lawful decision.

## 05.3 Joining and induction

The onboarding checklist shall cover identity verification, payroll setup, equipment, access, emergency contacts, policy orientation and role-specific training. Site access and task authorization are separate approvals. Completion of general induction shall not alone authorize operational work.

## 05.4 Initial integration

SAMPLE: managers shall agree initial objectives within 10 working days and hold a first-month check-in. HR shall monitor incomplete onboarding actions. Medical details shall remain with authorized health personnel; managers shall receive only necessary fitness or accommodation information.

Control record: policy owner - HR; approval - designated authority; evidence - relevant request, assessment, approval and employee communication.

# 06 | Working time, attendance and shifts

## 06.1 Schedules

HR, Operations and Legal shall approve working arrangements before publication. Schedules shall identify working periods, rest arrangements, weekly rest and any applicable seasonal or statutory adjustments. This manual does not assume one universal schedule for office and operational employees. Required limits and exceptions shall be documented in the legally validated schedule.

## 06.2 Attendance

Employees shall record attendance accurately and notify the supervisor as soon as practicable when delayed or absent. Managers shall investigate discrepancies before making a finding. System outages shall use an approved alternative record. Employees shall be able to request correction and review of disputed entries.

## 06.3 Shift control and handover

Operations shall publish rosters with adequate planning notice and maintain competent coverage. Shift exchanges require approval before implementation. Handovers shall identify staffing, outstanding tasks and relevant operating concerns through the approved operational process. Employees shall not abandon a safety-critical post; unresolved relief arrangements shall be escalated without requiring unsafe extended duty.

## 06.4 Overtime and fatigue

Overtime shall be authorized and recorded, including emergency work. Lack of prior approval shall trigger review but shall not automatically extinguish a lawful payment entitlement. Managers shall assess cumulative duty and fatigue and arrange relief where necessary. Remote or flexible working may be approved only for suitable roles and shall not bypass site-presence requirements.

Control record: policy owner - HR; approval - designated authority; evidence - relevant request, assessment, approval and employee communication.

# 07 | Leave and absence

## 07.1 Entitlement control

HR shall publish a legally validated leave schedule covering annual, public-holiday, sickness, family-related, bereavement, study, pilgrimage and other applicable leave categories. For each category the schedule shall define duration, pay treatment, qualifying conditions, evidence, notice and interaction with other leave. No unverified legal number is established by this sample.

## 07.2 Annual leave planning

Employees and managers shall plan leave to support rest and competent coverage. Requests shall identify dates and required handover arrangements. Managers shall give a reasoned response and propose alternatives when operational conflicts arise. Refusal shall not become indefinite denial of a legal entitlement. HR shall monitor unused balances and lawful carryover arrangements.

## 07.3 Unexpected absence

Employees shall notify the designated contact as soon as reasonably practicable. Where the employee cannot communicate, a representative may notify the Company. HR shall request only proportionate supporting evidence. Medical information shall be handled confidentially and not circulated with general attendance records.

## 07.4 Changes and disputes

Cancellation, recall, advance leave, unpaid leave and carryover require the approvals stated in the final schedule. The Company shall assess reasonable documented costs caused by an employer-directed change under an approved rule. Employees may challenge balance errors through HR. Unexplained absence shall be assessed through a fair process, not treated automatically as resignation.

Control record: policy owner - HR; approval - designated authority; evidence - relevant request, assessment, approval and employee communication.

# 08 | Pay, payroll and overtime

## 08.1 Pay structure

Compensation shall be based on an approved grade structure and documented pay components. Offers, salary changes and allowances require the prescribed approvals before payroll entry. No salary increase or bonus shall be guaranteed solely by a performance rating unless the approved scheme expressly provides it.

## 08.2 Payroll administration

Payroll shall operate to a published calendar with controlled input deadlines, verification and independent release approval. HR shall authorize employment changes; payroll shall calculate; Finance shall reconcile and release through authorized controls. Employees shall receive an understandable payslip and a confidential query channel.

## 08.3 Corrections and deductions

Reported underpayments shall be assessed promptly and corrected through an approved payment route. Overpayments shall be explained and any recovery shall follow lawful limits and a documented arrangement where required. No disputed deduction shall be imposed merely because a manager requests it. Required statutory contributions and registrations shall be validated by the responsible specialist.

## 08.4 Overtime and final controls

Overtime calculations shall use legally validated eligibility, rates and wage components. Time records shall distinguish ordinary work, overtime, on-call availability and actual call-out work where relevant. Payroll changes shall have an audit trail. SAMPLE: employees should report an apparent payroll error within five working days where possible; late reporting does not remove a lawful claim.

Control record: policy owner - HR; approval - designated authority; evidence - relevant request, assessment, approval and employee communication.

# 09 | Benefits and employee services

## 09.1 Eligibility

Benefits shall be governed by the approved eligibility schedule and the employment contract. The schedule shall specify employee category, grade or other lawful criteria, commencement, dependent eligibility, limits and cessation. Public descriptions of benefit categories shall not be treated as proof of an individual entitlement [R4].

## 09.2 Administration

HR shall provide written enrollment instructions and explain required evidence. Employees shall notify relevant changes accurately. HR shall verify eligibility without collecting unnecessary personal information. Insurer or provider terms shall be communicated, including exclusions and claim channels, without suggesting that an internal summary overrides mandatory coverage obligations.

## 09.3 Allowances and programs

Housing, transport, relocation, savings, recognition and incentive programs may be included only after formal approval. Each program shall define amount or formula, frequency, tax or statutory treatment where applicable, interaction with other benefits and termination treatment. No amounts are assumed in this sample.

## 09.4 Queries and changes

Employees may request a written eligibility explanation and correction of errors. Changes to providers or schemes shall be assessed for contractual and legal impact before implementation. Discretionary programs shall be identified clearly, but that label shall not be used to defeat accrued or enforceable rights.

Control record: policy owner - HR; approval - designated authority; evidence - relevant request, assessment, approval and employee communication.

# 10 | Business travel and expenses

## 10.1 Authorization

Business travel shall have an approved purpose, itinerary, cost estimate and budget owner before booking, except a documented emergency. The approved travel schedule shall set booking class, accommodation limits, meal treatment, ground transport and permitted exceptions. These values remain unapproved in this sample.

## 10.2 Booking and safety

Employees shall use approved booking channels where available. The responsible function shall assess destination requirements, emergency contacts and reasonable accessibility needs. Travel arrangements shall consider rest and fitness before safety-critical work. Personal extensions require approval and clear allocation of additional cost.

## 10.3 Claims

Claims shall identify the business purpose, dates, currency and supporting receipts. Duplicate reimbursement, personal expenditure and unsupported claims are prohibited unless an authorized exception addresses genuinely unavailable evidence. SAMPLE: claims should be submitted within 15 working days after return, subject to reasonable exceptions and applicable rights.

## 10.4 Review and settlement

Managers shall verify business purpose; Finance shall verify arithmetic, limits and duplicate claims. Rejections shall identify the reason and correction route. Travel advances shall be reconciled against actual eligible costs. No employee shall approve their own claim. Recovery of an unsettled advance shall follow lawful procedures rather than an automatic payroll deduction.

Control record: policy owner - HR; approval - designated authority; evidence - relevant request, assessment, approval and employee communication.

# 11 | Performance management

## 11.1 Objectives

Managers and employees shall agree role-relevant objectives and expected behaviors at the start of the review period. Objectives shall be measurable where practicable and updated when responsibilities materially change. Operational targets shall not encourage bypassing safety, quality or compliance requirements.

## 11.2 Review cycle

SAMPLE: the cycle shall include annual objectives, a midyear discussion and a year-end review, supported by regular feedback. The employee shall have an opportunity to provide evidence and comments. Ratings shall reflect the full period, relevant circumstances and documented expectations, not a single recent event.

## 11.3 Calibration and appeal

HR shall facilitate calibration for consistency without imposing an undisclosed predetermined distribution. Managers shall explain the rating and development priorities. Employees may request review by a manager not responsible for the original decision. A disagreement alone shall not be treated as misconduct.

## 11.4 Improvement support

Where performance is below expectation, the manager shall identify the gap, evidence, required standard, support and review dates. A written improvement plan shall allow a reasonable opportunity to improve, adjusted to the role and circumstances. Capability, health-related restrictions and misconduct shall be assessed separately. Any employment consequence requires HR and legal review as appropriate.

Control record: policy owner - HR; approval - designated authority; evidence - relevant request, assessment, approval and employee communication.

# 12 | Learning, competence and succession

## 12.1 Development planning

Managers shall identify development needs from role requirements, performance discussions, changes in technology and future staffing plans. HR shall consolidate priorities and budget. Training approval shall identify the learning objective, cost, employee availability and expected application.

## 12.2 Mandatory competence

Operations and HSE shall define mandatory training, assessment and authorization for safety-critical tasks. Attendance alone shall not prove competence. Expired certification or an unsuccessful assessment shall trigger a controlled restriction and a development or reassessment plan, not informal continuation of restricted duties.

## 12.3 Records and evaluation

HR or the designated learning function shall maintain completion, assessment and expiry records. Managers shall confirm whether learning has been applied. External qualifications shall be verified where relevant. Access to learning shall follow transparent, role-related criteria.

## 12.4 Succession and education support

Critical-role succession plans shall identify readiness, gaps and development actions; inclusion does not guarantee promotion. Education assistance or training repayment arrangements require separate approved terms and legal review before commitment. The Company shall not assume that every training cost can lawfully be recovered from an employee.

Control record: policy owner - HR; approval - designated authority; evidence - relevant request, assessment, approval and employee communication.

# 13 | Promotion, transfer and temporary assignment

## 13.1 Promotion

Promotion shall require an approved role or formally approved change in responsibility, evidence of readiness and the prescribed budget and HR review. Strong performance in the current role alone does not create an automatic entitlement to promotion. Selection criteria and decisions shall be documented.

## 13.2 Transfers

Transfers shall consider business need, employee competence, contract terms, location and applicable consent requirements. HR shall coordinate effective dates, pay implications, leave records and handover. A transfer shall not be used to punish an employee for raising a concern.

## 13.3 Acting assignments

Temporary assignments shall be confirmed in writing with scope, reporting line, duration, authority and any approved allowance. SAMPLE: assignments shall be reviewed at least every 90 calendar days to avoid indefinite informal arrangements. Acting in a role does not automatically authorize safety-critical work or guarantee permanent appointment.

## 13.4 Handover and communication

The releasing and receiving managers shall agree transition actions and access changes. Employees shall receive written confirmation of material changes before implementation where required. HR shall update the position and employee records and notify payroll only after approval.

Control record: policy owner - HR; approval - designated authority; evidence - relevant request, assessment, approval and employee communication.

# 14 | Conduct, conflicts and company resources

## 14.1 Professional conduct

Employees shall act honestly, treat colleagues respectfully, follow lawful instructions and protect company information and assets. Bullying, intimidation, discriminatory treatment and retaliation are prohibited. Managers shall model expected behavior and address concerns consistently.

## 14.2 Conflicts and gifts

Employees shall disclose personal, financial or outside-work interests that could affect impartial judgment. The Company shall assess whether recusal, reassignment or another control is needed. Gifts and hospitality shall follow a separately approved register and thresholds; cash or benefits intended to influence a decision are prohibited.

## 14.3 Information and resources

Company systems, equipment and access shall be used for authorized purposes. Employees shall not share credentials or bypass access controls. Confidential information shall not be entered into unapproved external tools, including AI services. Reasonable-use and monitoring rules shall be communicated and legally reviewed.

## 14.4 External communications

Only authorized spokespersons may speak on behalf of the Company. Personal public statements shall not falsely imply company endorsement or disclose protected information. Nothing in this chapter shall prevent lawful reporting to a competent authority or another protected exercise of employee rights.

Control record: policy owner - HR; approval - designated authority; evidence - relevant request, assessment, approval and employee communication.

# 15 | Health, safety, fitness and wellbeing

## 15.1 Interface with safety rules

This chapter establishes employment responsibilities and does not replace technical safety procedures, permit systems or emergency plans. Employees shall complete required induction, use required protective equipment and follow role-specific controls. Supervisors shall not direct work outside a person's authorization or competence.

## 15.2 Unsafe conditions and fatigue

Employees shall stop or refrain from work they reasonably believe presents an immediate serious risk and notify the designated supervisor through the approved safety process. Managers shall arrange assessment and safe alternatives without retaliation for good-faith reporting. Fatigue concerns shall be evaluated in the context of duty history and task risk.

## 15.3 Fitness and support

Fitness assessments shall be job-related, proportionate and handled by authorized health professionals. Managers shall receive only necessary work restrictions or accommodations, not unrestricted medical records. HR shall coordinate reasonable support and return-to-work arrangements with the employee and relevant specialists.

## 15.4 Incidents and wellbeing

Work-related incidents shall be reported through the approved HSE process, with HR coordinating employment and benefit administration. Access to wellbeing support shall be confidential within explained limits. Emergency response shall use actual published site contacts; this sample intentionally supplies no invented emergency number.

Control record: policy owner - HR; approval - designated authority; evidence - relevant request, assessment, approval and employee communication.

# 16 | Employee concerns and grievances

## 16.1 Access and protection

Employees may raise concerns with their manager, HR or another designated independent channel. A concern involving the manager may bypass that manager. Good-faith reporting, participation in a review and requesting a policy explanation shall not result in retaliation. Anonymous concerns shall be assessed on the available information.

## 16.2 Intake and assessment

HR shall record the issue, requested resolution, immediate risks and any conflict of interest. Urgent safety, safeguarding, privacy or evidence-preservation issues shall be referred promptly to the competent function. Informal resolution may be offered where appropriate but shall not be forced.

## 16.3 Review and response

An impartial reviewer shall gather relevant information and allow affected persons to respond. Confidentiality shall be limited to those who need the information; absolute secrecy shall not be promised. SAMPLE: acknowledge within two working days and aim for an initial substantive response within 10 working days. Where more time is needed, explain the reason and next update date.

## 16.4 Appeal and closure

The employee shall receive a reasoned outcome to the extent consistent with others' privacy. An appeal shall be reviewed by an uninvolved authority. SAMPLE: request appeal within 10 working days, with reasonable extensions. Internal procedures shall not restrict access to competent external authorities or shorten statutory rights.

Control record: policy owner - HR; approval - designated authority; evidence - relevant request, assessment, approval and employee communication.

# 17 | Investigations and disciplinary decisions

## 17.1 Fair process

Allegations shall be assessed without presuming guilt. HR shall confirm the applicable approved work regulations, legal requirements and disciplinary schedule before formal action. The employee shall receive sufficient information to respond and a reasonable opportunity to present relevant evidence.

## 17.2 Investigation controls

An investigator shall disclose conflicts and maintain an evidence log. Interviews shall be documented accurately and relevant records preserved lawfully. Access to evidence shall be proportionate. Any temporary reassignment or suspension requires legal and HR review of authority, pay treatment, duration and employee communication; it is not a finding of misconduct.

## 17.3 Decision

The authorized decision-maker shall assess evidence, seriousness, consistency, mitigating factors and applicable legal limits. No automatic fine, deduction or dismissal is created by this sample. The outcome shall state the finding, reasons, any authorized measure, effective date and appeal route. Capability concerns shall not be relabeled as misconduct to avoid appropriate support.

## 17.4 Appeal and retention

An uninvolved authority shall review an appeal and may uphold, amend or overturn the decision within its lawful authority. HR shall retain records under an approved schedule with restricted access. An unsubstantiated complaint is not itself evidence of bad faith; any allegation of deliberate fabrication requires separate evidence and fair assessment.

Control record: policy owner - HR; approval - designated authority; evidence - relevant request, assessment, approval and employee communication.

# 18 | Employee privacy and records

## 18.1 Privacy principles

HR shall identify a lawful basis and specific purpose for each employee-data activity and provide an understandable privacy notice. Data shall be limited to what is necessary, protected against unauthorized access and retained only for justified periods, consistent with the public principles described by the Saudi Data and Artificial Intelligence Authority [R3]. Consent shall not be assumed to be the correct basis for every employment activity.

## 18.2 Access and segregation

Personnel, payroll, investigation and medical records shall have separate role-based access where appropriate. Managers shall receive information necessary for their responsibilities, not unrestricted access to the employee file. Access shall be reviewed after role changes and removed when no longer required.

## 18.3 Rights and service providers

Employees shall have a documented channel to request access, correction or other applicable rights. The privacy function shall validate identity and respond within applicable legal timelines. Providers shall be assessed and bound by appropriate contractual safeguards. Any international transfer shall receive the required legal and privacy assessment before implementation.

## 18.4 Retention and incidents

A record-specific schedule shall define retention trigger, period, legal basis, owner and disposal method. Legal holds shall suspend relevant disposal. Suspected loss or unauthorized disclosure shall be reported immediately to the designated incident channel. The privacy and security functions shall assess containment and any notification obligations; no unverified statutory deadline is invented here.

Control record: policy owner - HR; approval - designated authority; evidence - relevant request, assessment, approval and employee communication.

# 19 | Contractor and secondee interfaces

## 19.1 Employment boundary

The contract owner shall document the employing entity, supervision arrangements and division of responsibilities. Contractor personnel remain subject to their employer's employment terms except where applicable law or a specific agreement provides otherwise. Company site rules shall not be confused with direct-employee benefit eligibility.

## 19.2 Site requirements

Contractor and secondee access shall require appropriate authorization, induction, competence verification and security controls. The Company may restrict unsafe or unauthorized site activity through approved procedures. Employment discipline or pay decisions shall be referred to the actual employer and managed lawfully.

## 19.3 Secondments

A written agreement shall address duration, reporting, pay administration, leave coordination, performance input, confidentiality, insurance, return arrangements and applicable employment obligations. HR and Legal shall review ambiguities before the assignment begins.

## 19.4 Concerns and exit

Personnel shall be told how to report site safety, conduct and access concerns without retaliation. The contract owner shall coordinate the relevant employer's response. End-of-assignment clearance shall cover access, assets and information without misrepresenting it as a direct-employee final settlement.

Control record: policy owner - HR; approval - designated authority; evidence - relevant request, assessment, approval and employee communication.

# 20 | Separation, retirement and offboarding

## 20.1 Initiation and review

HR shall classify the proposed separation route accurately, including resignation, contract expiry, retirement, mutual agreement or employer-initiated termination. Legal shall review high-risk or disputed cases and confirm applicable grounds, notice, process and payment obligations. No universal notice period or retirement age is assumed by this sample.

## 20.2 Employee communication

Communications shall state the relevant dates, next steps, contact person and applicable review or appeal route. Resignation receipt shall be acknowledged and handled under the applicable legal process; the manager shall not invent a different acceptance rule. Contract renewals and expiry decisions shall be tracked early enough to meet required deadlines.

## 20.3 Handover and access

The manager shall arrange knowledge transfer and asset return. IT and Security shall coordinate access removal to the actual departure and risk context, preserving necessary evidence and business records lawfully. Clearance tasks shall not automatically justify withholding legally due payments.

## 20.4 Final settlement and closure

Payroll shall prepare an itemized calculation of outstanding pay, eligible leave, applicable end-of-service amounts, lawful deductions and other entitlements using validated rules. HR and Finance shall independently review it and meet applicable payment deadlines. Employees shall receive required documents and a query channel. Exit feedback is voluntary and shall be analyzed without retaliation.

Control record: policy owner - HR; approval - designated authority; evidence - relevant request, assessment, approval and employee communication.

# 21 | HR services, assurance and implementation

## 21.1 Service channels

HR shall publish actual service contacts, request channels, escalation owners and accessibility arrangements before launch. Requests shall receive a reference number where feasible. Service targets shall be distinguished from statutory deadlines; an internal target cannot extend a legal deadline.

## 21.2 Quality assurance

HR shall review payroll corrections, overdue cases, unused leave, training expiries, policy exceptions and appeal outcomes. Reports shall use aggregate information where possible and avoid unnecessary exposure of employee data. Patterns of inconsistent treatment shall trigger corrective action and manager coaching.

## 21.3 Implementation

Before adoption, the Company shall validate the legal register, approve authority limits, complete benefit schedules, test forms and workflows, configure systems and train managers. Existing contracts and accrued rights shall be reviewed before transition. Employees shall receive the effective version and a practical explanation of material changes.

## 21.4 Maintenance

The policy owner shall review this manual at least annually as a proposed governance practice, and sooner following relevant legal or organizational change. Each amendment shall record the reason, reviewer, approver, effective date and communication evidence. Obsolete copies shall be withdrawn while prior versions remain available for legitimate historical reference.
